Basic instructions
==================

See the help text in `the app <https://infinity0.github.io/lcra/>`_.

Other functions
===============

- You can hide the article panel using the (⇤) button, and instead directly read an article in another window. Then, select words there, and drag them onto the add (⊕) button.
- You can also input new words by clicking (⊕) when nothing is selected.
- You can select several words and add them all at once, if they are separated by spaces or newlines. Separating normal Chinese text into words is almost as hard as translating them entirely, something we cannot achieve in this simple program.
- Double-click the Pinyin box to reset it to our autogenerated Pinyin for the selected word. (It may be wrong.)

Guide to references
===================

All references have occasional mistakes and incompleteness. The only true definition of Chinese is what is actually spoken by Chinese people. Seek truth from reality, don't rely on just a few sources or books!

Purple Culture, HanziCraft
  - Good set of basic info for each item, beginner friendly.
  - Not super up-to-date on the latest slang, memes, or tech.
  - HanziCraft can only look up single characters, Purple Culture can also look up multi-character words.
  - PurpleCulture can only look up a character's constituents (decomposing), HanziCraft can also look up its derivatives (composing).

Wiktionary
  - Very detailed, probably overwhelming for a beginner. Includes etymology, radical categories, phonetic series, pronounciation in different dialects, etc.
  - Contains some niche words and phrases missing from Purple Culture and HanziCraft.

Baidu Fanyi
  - Contains the latest slang, memes, tech, niche phrases, celebrity names, etc, that are missing from traditional dictionaries.
  - Can help break down complex sentences into its constituent words and phrases.
  - Can help guess the meaning of rare technical jargon and hapax legomena.
  - Dictionary results are taken from Chinese-Chinese dictionaries, and sometimes do not include English definitions. (Translation results are always in English.)
  - Slower than Google Translate, Bing Translate, due to "security" throttling our app.

Baidu Baike
  - Contains the latest slang, memes, tech, niche phrases, celebrity names, etc, that are missing from traditional dictionaries.
  - As a wiki-like encyclopedia, contains detailed background information about each topic, in Chinese.

Baidu Search
  - Similar for language learning purposes to Baidu Baike, but more complete, yet less structured.
  - Sometimes slower than Baidu Baike, due to "security" throttling our app.

Google Translate, Bing Translate
  - Similar to Baidu Fanyi, but less complete for Chinese cultural phrases or other concepts that are less common in western culture.
  - Faster than Baidu Fanyi, due to less/no "security" throttling.
